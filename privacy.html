1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumen ("Lumen," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use our website and app (the "Service"). By using Lumen, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Your name or display name, and your affirmation of Lumen's Statement of Faith when you join.
  • Content you post or share, including prayer requests, testimonies, discussion messages in Bible study groups, and prayer-partner check-ins.
  • Preferences you provide, such as prayer focus and availability, used for prayer-partner matching.
  • Any messages you send to us directly, such as support or contact requests.

Information collected automatically: basic technical and usage information such as browser type, device type, and general interaction with the Service, used to keep Lumen working reliably and to understand how features are used.

Video calls: group and prayer-partner video calls are powered by Jitsi Meet, a third-party video calling service, not owned or operated by Lumen. Your audio and video during a call are transmitted through Jitsi's infrastructure rather than stored on Lumen's servers. Jitsi's handling of call data is governed by Jitsi's own privacy practices, which we encourage you to review.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and provide the Prayer Wall, Bible Study Groups, Prayer Partner matching, and Testimony Feed features.
  • To match you with a prayer partner or relevant groups based on the preferences you share.
  • To personalize your experience, such as showing the verse of the day.
  • To maintain community safety, including reviewing reported content and enforcing our Terms of Service.
  • To communicate with you about your account or the Service.
  • To improve and maintain the Service.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Religious and Spiritual Information

Using Lumen inherently involves sharing religious beliefs and deeply personal spiritual content — prayer requests, testimonies, and your affirmation of our Statement of Faith. By using Lumen, you consent to this information being processed as described in this policy. If you'd like more privacy on a given post, the Prayer Wall supports posting anonymously.

5. How Information Is Shared

  • With other members: content you post to the Prayer Wall or Testimony Feed is visible to the Lumen community (unless posted anonymously); group discussion is visible to members of that group; prayer-partner check-ins are visible to you and your matched partner.
  • With service providers: vendors who help us run Lumen, such as hosting providers and Jitsi Meet for video calls.
  • For legal or safety reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Lumen, our members, or the public.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service. You can edit or delete your own prayer requests, testimonies, and discussion messages at any time from within the app. You may request full deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see Section 12).

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 12.

8. Children's Privacy

Lumen is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are between 13 and 18, you should have a parent or legal guardian's permission to use Lumen. If we learn we've collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Local Storage and Cookies

Lumen uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember your activity on the current device. We may use cookies or similar technologies as needed for the Service to function.
